# User Guide - Android Shop App

Welcome to the **Android Shop App**! This guide will help you understand how to use and navigate the application.

## 1. Introduction
The Android Shop App is a modern e-commerce platform designed for a seamless shopping experience. It features a wide variety of products, a persistent shopping cart, and a user-friendly interface.

## 2. Getting Started
When you first open the app, you will land on the **Home Screen** (Catalog).

### Navigating the App
The app uses a bottom navigation bar for easy access to different sections:
- **Shop**: Browse all available products.
- **Cart**: View items you have added and proceed to checkout.
- **Profile**: Manage your account and view order history.

## 3. Features

### Browsing Products
- **List/Grid Toggle**: You can switch between a detailed list view and a compact grid view.
- **Product Details**: Tap on any product card to see more details, including a full description and higher-resolution images.
- **Search**: Use the search bar at the top to find specific items.

### Shopping Cart
- **Adding Items**: Tap the "Add to Cart" button on any product.
- **Quantity Management**: In the cart, you can increase or decrease the quantity of each item using the `+` and `-` buttons.
- **Removing Items**: Use the trash icon to remove an item from your cart.
- **Persistence**: Your cart is saved even if you close the app!

### User Account
- **Login/Register**: Securely sign in to save your preferences and orders.
- **Profile**: View your personal information and change settings like Dark Mode.

## 4. Settings
The app automatically follows your system's theme (Light or Dark). You can also toggle this manually in the Profile section.

## 5. FAQ

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes, all data is stored locally using Room or securely transmitted to our mock API.

**Q: How do I checkout?**
A: Go to your Cart, tap the "Checkout" button, and follow the on-screen instructions to enter your shipping details.
